With one thread, each insertion finishes before the next operation begins, so
the bucket head cannot change between the lookup and the update.  With two
threads, suppose both choose the same bucket and read the same old head H.
Thread A allocates A, sets A->next to H, and stores A in the bucket head.
Thread B, which still holds its stale snapshot H, then allocates B, sets
B->next to H, and stores B in the bucket head.  B's final store overwrites A's
head update, making A and its key unreachable.  A mutex per bucket makes the
lookup-and-insert sequence atomic while still allowing operations on different
buckets to proceed in parallel.
